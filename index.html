🔑 Getting Your Free Gemini API Key
1
Open Google AI Studio in your browser
Visit aistudio.google.com/app/apikey — this is Google's free platform for AI. No signup for the platform is required, just a Google account.
2
Sign in with any Google / Gmail account
Click the Sign in button and log in with your Gmail. Don't have one? Create a free Google account here. It takes 2 minutes and requires no credit card.
3
Click "Create API key"
You'll see a blue "Create API key" button. Click it, then choose "Create API key in new project" from the dropdown. Google will generate your key instantly — it looks like AIzaSyABC123xyzDEF456...
4
Copy and paste your key below
Click the copy icon next to your key, then paste it into the Gemini API Key field below. The status dot will turn green when the key format is recognised. That's it — you're ready!
5
Free limits & what models are used
The free tier supports Gemini Flash models (1.5-flash, 2.0-flash, 2.5-flash etc.). This tool auto-picks the newest Flash model available on your key — fast, accurate vision extraction, totally free. Flash is well-suited for table and chart OCR.
💡 Privacy note: Your API key is never stored or sent anywhere except directly to Google's servers. This tool runs 100% in your browser. Treat your key like a password — never post it in public places or upload it to GitHub.
